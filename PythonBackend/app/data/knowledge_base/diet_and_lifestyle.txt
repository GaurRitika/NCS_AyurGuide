AYURVEDIC DIETARY PRINCIPLES (AHARA) AND LIFESTYLE ROUTINES (DINACHARYA)

FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLES OF AYURVEDIC NUTRITION (AHARA)
In Ayurveda, food is medicine when chosen according to one's unique constitution (Prakriti), current imbalance (Vikriti), digestive capacity (Agni), and the season (Ritu).
1. Digestive Fire (Agni): Good health depends on balanced Agni. Weak digestion creates Ama (toxic metabolic residue), which is the root cause of systemic illnesses.
2. The Six Tastes (Shad Rasa): Every complete meal should ideally include all six tastes: Sweet (Madhura), Sour (Amla), Salty (Lavana), Pungent (Katu), Bitter (Tikta), and Astringent (Kashaya).
3. Mindful Eating: Eat in a calm, seated environment without screens or emotional disturbance. Fill 1/3 of the stomach with solid food, 1/3 with liquid, and leave 1/3 empty for digestion. Avoid drinking large amounts of ice water during meals; sip small amounts of warm water or ginger tea instead.

VATA PACIFYING DIET AND REGIMEN
Vata is cold, dry, light, rough, and mobile. To balance Vata, favor foods that are warm, moist, grounding, nourishing, and unctuous (healthy fats).
Foods to Favor:
- Well-cooked, warm foods: soups, stews, kitchari, cooked grains (oats, basmati rice, quinoa).
- Healthy oils and fats: warm ghee, sesame oil, olive oil, soaked almonds and walnuts.
- Sweet, sour, and salty tastes pacify Vata.
- Warming spices: fresh ginger, cumin, coriander, fennel, cinnamon, cardamom, hing (asafetida).
Foods to Avoid or Minimize:
- Cold, iced drinks, raw vegetables, dry crackers, cold salads, beans that produce gas, and bitter, astringent, and pungent tastes in excess.
Lifestyle Recommendations for Vata:
- Maintain a strict, predictable daily routine (regular times for waking, meals, and sleep).
- Daily self-massage (Abhyanga) with warm sesame oil before warm baths.
- Calming, grounding physical exercise: gentle Hatha yoga, walking, restorative stretching, and Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing).

PITTA PACIFYING DIET AND REGIMEN
Pitta is hot, sharp, light, oily, and spreading. To balance Pitta, favor foods that are cooling, calming, moderately heavy, and mildly dry.
Foods to Favor:
- Naturally sweet, bitter, and astringent tastes pacify Pitta.
- Cooling fruits: sweet apples, pears, melons, sweet berries, pomegranate, coconut water.
- Vegetables: leafy greens, zucchini, cucumber, asparagus, broccoli, sweet potatoes.
- Grains: basmati rice, barley, oats, wheat.
- Oils and dairy: cooling ghee, coconut oil, fresh milk, unsalted butter, cottage cheese.
- Mild cooling spices: coriander, fennel, cardamom, mint, fresh cilantro, turmeric in moderation.
Foods to Avoid or Minimize:
- Hot, spicy chilies, fermented foods, vinegar, pickles, deep-fried snacks, excessive garlic, onions, tomatoes, alcohol, coffee, and sour/salty items.
Lifestyle Recommendations for Pitta:
- Avoid excessive mid-day sun exposure and overheating environments.
- Practice cooling breathing techniques: Sheetali and Sheetkari pranayama.
- Abhyanga with cooling oils such as coconut oil or Brahmi oil.
- Exercise in the cooler parts of the day (early morning or dusk): swimming, moderate hiking, calming yoga.
- Cultivate leisure, playfulness, and emotional detachment from work stress.

KAPHA PACIFYING DIET AND REGIMEN
Kapha is heavy, cold, moist, slow, steady, and oily. To balance Kapha, favor foods that are warm, light, dry, stimulating, and easily digestible.
Foods to Favor:
- Pungent, bitter, and astringent tastes strongly pacify Kapha.
- Light grains: millet, buckwheat, barley, quinoa, corn.
- Legumes: mung beans, red lentils, chickpeas (well-spiced).
- Abundant steamed and sauteed vegetables: bitter greens, cabbage, cauliflower, radishes, peppers.
- Warming, stimulating spices: black pepper, ginger, cayenne, mustard seeds, fenugreek, cloves, cinnamon.
- Small amounts of old raw honey (honey should never be cooked or heated).
Foods to Avoid or Minimize:
- Heavy dairy (cheese, cold milk, ice cream), fried foods, excessive wheat, refined sugar, pastries, cold drinks, sweet, sour, and salty tastes in excess.
Lifestyle Recommendations for Kapha:
- Rise before sunrise (before 6:00 AM) to avoid morning heaviness and lethargy. Avoid daytime sleeping.
- Vigorous daily exercise: Surya Namaskara (Sun Salutations), brisk walking, running, cycling, or energetic yoga.
- Dry brushing (Garshana) with raw silk gloves or dry powder massage (Udvartana) to stimulate lymphatic flow.
- Kapalabhati and Bhastrika pranayama to stimulate internal metabolic fire.

DAILY ROUTINE (DINACHARYA) FOR RADIANT HEALTH
1. Brahma Muhurta: Wake up 45-60 minutes before sunrise when sattvic energy is highest.
2. Oral Hygiene: Scrape the tongue with a copper or stainless steel tongue scraper (Jihwa Nirlekhana) to remove toxins; oil pull (Gandusha) with sesame oil for oral health.
3. Warm Hydration: Drink 1-2 glasses of warm water (Ushnodaka) upon waking to stimulate downward peristalsis (Apana Vata).
4. Evacuation: Honor natural urges for urination and bowel movement promptly.
5. Movement and Pranayama: Practice 20-30 minutes of constitution-appropriate yoga and conscious breathwork.
6. Abhyanga: Nourish skin and nervous system with warm oil massage.
7. Sleep Schedule: Retire to bed by 10:00 PM before the Pitta nighttime cycle begins.
