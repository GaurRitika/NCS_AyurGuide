AYURVEDIC PERSPECTIVE AND MANAGEMENT OF COMMON HEALTH CONDITIONS

DIGESTIVE DISORDERS (AMINA / AJIRNA / AMLAPITTA)
In Ayurveda, almost all disease begins in the gastrointestinal tract due to impaired Agni and accumulation of Ama.
- Hyperacidity & Acid Reflux (Amlapitta): Primarily caused by aggravated Pitta dosha. Manifests as burning sensation in chest/throat, sour eructations, nausea, and headache.
  Therapeutic Management: Pacify Pitta. Favor cooling foods (coconut water, coriander-fennel tea, soaked raisins, aloe vera juice). Herbs: Shatavari, Yashtimadhu (Licorice), Amla, Kamadudha Rasa. Avoid sour, spicy, fried, and fermented foods. Do not skip meals or stay hungry for extended periods.
- Indigestion & Sluggish Digestion (Mandagni / Ajirna): Often caused by Kapha-Vata imbalance. Manifests as heaviness in the stomach, bloating, loss of appetite, and lethargy.
  Therapeutic Management: Kindle Agni using Trikatu (ginger, black pepper, long pepper), Hingwastak churna, or drinking warm water boiled with cumin, coriander, and fennel (CCF Tea). Chew a thin slice of fresh ginger with rock salt and lemon drops 10 minutes before meals.
- Constipation (Vibandha): Governed by aggravated Apana Vata (dryness and downward stagnation).
  Therapeutic Management: Hydrate with warm water, consume 1 teaspoon of warm cow's ghee in hot milk at bedtime, take 1 teaspoon Triphala churna in warm water at night, and avoid dry, light, and raw foods.

STRESS, ANXIETY, AND MENTAL FATIGUE (MANASIKA ROGA)
Mental stress and anxiety are primarily manifestations of aggravated Prana Vata and Raja guna, which deplete Ojas (vital immunity and emotional resilience).
Manifestations: Restless thoughts, palpitations, panic tendencies, tension headaches, physical tremors, and shallow breathing.
Therapeutic Management:
- Herbs: Ashwagandha (reduces cortisol and stabilizes nervous energy), Brahmi / Bacopa (cools and clears the mental faculties), Shankhpushpi, and Jatamansi.
- Therapies: Shirodhara (warm herbal oil gently poured across the third eye/forehead), Abhyanga with warm Mahanarayan or sesame oil, and Pada Abhyanga (foot massage with warm oil before sleep).
- Lifestyle & Mind: Practice Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing) for 10-15 minutes twice daily. Practice Bhramari pranayama (humming bee breath) to soothe the vagus nerve. Establish a grounded daily routine and avoid stimulants like coffee, energy drinks, and excessive screen time before bed.

SLEEP DISORDERS AND INSOMNIA (ANIDRA)
Insomnia in Ayurveda is caused by elevated Vata and Pitta invading the mental and sensory channels (Mano Vaha Srotas), preventing Tarpaka Kapha from nourishing the brain.
Manifestations: Difficulty falling asleep, frequent nighttime awakenings with racing thoughts (Vata), or waking up hot and sweating around 2-3 AM (Pitta).
Therapeutic Management:
- Bedtime Elixir: Warm milk (or almond milk) boiled with 1/2 tsp Ashwagandha, a pinch of nutmeg (Jaiphal), cardamom, and 1/2 tsp ghee taken 30 minutes before bed. Nutmeg acts as a gentle natural Ayurvedic sedative.
- Oil Application: Massage warm sesame or Brahmi oil into the crown of the head (Murrdha thaila) and soles of the feet (Padabhyanga).
- Routine: Turn off screens by 9:00 PM. Sleep before 10:00 PM to catch the restful Kapha sleep window before Pitta activates at 10:00 PM.

JOINT PAIN AND MUSCULOSKELETAL DISORDERS (SANDHIVATA / AMAVATA)
- Osteoarthritis (Sandhivata): Degenerative Vata condition characterized by dryness and loss of synovial fluid (Shleshaka Kapha) in the joints. Symptoms include crepitation, cracking joints, stiffness, and pain aggravated by cold, dry weather.
  Therapeutic Management: Internal and external Snehana (oleation). Drink warm milk with turmeric and 1 tsp ghee; consume Ashwagandha and Guggulu formulations (Yograj Guggulu). Warm oil massage (Abhyanga) using Mahanarayan Taila followed by warm moist compresses (Swedana).
- Rheumatoid Arthritis (Amavata): Toxic autoimmune accumulation where Ama combines with Vata in the joint spaces, creating intense inflammation, swelling, heat, and pain.
  Therapeutic Management: Must NOT apply heavy oils during acute inflammation. First digest Ama with hot spices (ginger, castor oil with ginger tea, Shallaki / Boswellia, Punarnavadi Kwath). Once Ama is cleared, gentle dry heat (Valuka Sweda - warm sand bag compress) is beneficial.

METABOLIC HEALTH AND WEIGHT IMBALANCE (STHOULYA / PRAMEHA)
Excess weight and blood sugar dysregulation involve sluggish Meda Dhatu (adipose tissue) metabolism and elevated Kapha.
Therapeutic Management:
- Kindle Medo-dhatvagni (fat metabolism fire) with warm water, Triphala, Guggulu, and Shilajit.
- Herbs: Vijaysar (Pterocarpus marsupium), Jamun seed powder, Gurmar (Gymnema sylvestre / destroyer of sugar), and Turmeric with Amla (Nisha-Amalaki combination).
- Diet: Favor barley (Yava), millets, bitter gourd (Karela), and fenugreek seeds soaked in warm water. Strictly avoid refined sugars, heavy cold desserts, fried foods, and daytime sleep.

SKIN DISORDERS AND ALLERGIES (KUSHTHA / SHEETAPITTA)
Skin conditions reflect imbalance in Rakta Dhatu (blood tissue) and Pitta dosha, often aggravated by Kapha.
Manifestations: Eczema, psoriasis, urticaria (hives), acne, redness, and itching.
Therapeutic Management:
- Blood Purification (Raktashodhana): Herbs such as Neem, Manjistha, Guduchi, Khadirarishta, and Sarivadyasava.
- Diet: Strictly eliminate sour, salty, and pungent excess (citrus, tomatoes, vinegar, fermented foods). Favor cooling foods, leafy greens, bitter melons, and coconut water.
- Topical Care: Apply pure Aloe Vera gel or Mahatiktaka Ghrita topically to soothe burning and itching.
