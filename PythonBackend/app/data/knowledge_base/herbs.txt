AYURVEDIC HERBS AND CLASSICAL PHARMACOPEIA (DRAVYAGUNA)

TRIPHALA (Haritaki, Bibhitaki, Amalaki)
Triphala is the premier tridoshic formulation in Ayurveda, balancing Vata, Pitta, and Kapha equally. It contains all tastes (Rasas) except salty: sweet, sour, pungent, bitter, and astringent.
Properties: Mildly warming virya, sweet vipaka, tridoshic.
Therapeutic Benefits: Gently cleanses and tones the gastrointestinal tract, eliminates Ama (metabolic toxins), supports healthy bowel movement without creating dependency, purifies blood, and rejuvenates bodily tissues (Rasayana).
Dosage & Administration: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon (3-5 grams) of Triphala churna (powder) stirred into warm water, taken before bed or first thing in the morning. For Pitta, take with warm water or ghee; for Kapha, with raw honey; for Vata, with warm water or sesame oil.
Contraindications: Avoid in acute diarrhea, severe dehydration, or pregnancy without medical guidance.

ASHWAGANDHA (Withania somnifera / Indian Ginseng)
Ashwagandha is a primary adaptogen and Rasayana herb renowned for balancing Vata and Kapha while calming the nervous system (Majja Dhatu).
Properties: Bitter and astringent rasa, heating virya, sweet vipaka.
Therapeutic Benefits: Strengthens nervous system, relieves chronic fatigue, reduces cortisol, alleviates anxiety, enhances restful sleep (treating Anidra), enhances muscle tone and physical stamina (Ojas builder), and supports adrenal and reproductive health.
Dosage & Administration: 3-6 grams daily of root powder mixed in warm milk or warm water with a pinch of nutmeg and ghee before bedtime.
Contraindications: Avoid in severe Pitta flare-ups, high systemic heat, severe congestion with high Ama, or active hyperthyroidism without supervision.

BRAHMI & GOTU KOLA (Bacopa monnieri / Centella asiatica)
Brahmi is Ayurveda's foremost medhya rasayana (brain and nervous system tonic) specifically targeted to cool Pitta and calm agitated Vata.
Properties: Bitter and sweet rasa, cooling virya, sweet vipaka; balances Pitta and Vata.
Therapeutic Benefits: Enhances memory (Smriti), intellect (Dhi), and concentration (Dhriti). Relieves mental fatigue, pacifies heat in the head, calms angry or agitated emotions, aids in restful sleep, and reduces headache from stress or heat.
Dosage & Administration: 250-500 mg extract or 1/2 to 1 teaspoon powder with warm water, ghee, or milk twice daily after meals.
Contraindications: Safe for long-term use; excessive doses on an empty stomach may cause mild nausea in sensitive individuals.

SHATAVARI (Asparagus racemosus)
Shatavari is the premier nourishing and rejuvenating tonic for Pitta and Vata, renowned for reproductive, hormonal, and digestive vitality.
Properties: Sweet and bitter rasa, cooling virya, sweet vipaka; pacifies Pitta and Vata, mildly increases Kapha.
Therapeutic Benefits: Soothes inflamed mucous membranes, calms gastric acidity (Amlapitta) and stomach ulcers, balances female and male reproductive hormones, nourishes Shukra and Rasa dhatus, and strengthens immunity.
Dosage & Administration: 3-5 grams powder taken with warm milk, ghee, or warm water twice daily.
Contraindications: Avoid in states of extreme Kapha congestion, high mucus production, or edema.

TULSI (Holy Basil / Ocimum sanctum)
Tulsi is a sacred sattvic herb that balances Vata and Kapha while purifying the subtle and physical respiratory channels (Pranavaha Srotas).
Properties: Pungent and bitter rasa, warming virya, pungent vipaka; reduces Kapha and Vata.
Therapeutic Benefits: Clears mucus, relieves cough and cold, enhances lung capacity, supports cardiovascular circulation, elevates mood, and bolsters Ojas and cellular immunity.
Dosage & Administration: Fresh leaves chewed or 1-2 teaspoons dried herb brewed as herbal tea 2-3 times daily.
Contraindications: Moderation during severe Pitta heat or bleeding conditions due to warming potency.

AMLA / AMALAKI (Emblica officinalis / Indian Gooseberry)
Amalaki is one of the richest natural sources of vitamin C and a potent tridoshic Rasayana, specially pacifying Pitta dosha.
Properties: Sour, sweet, pungent, bitter, astringent rasas (5 of 6 tastes); cooling virya, sweet vipaka.
Therapeutic Benefits: Cools internal heat, balances stomach acid (Agni deepana without increasing Pitta), nourishes hair, eyes (Chakshushya), and liver, and protects against cellular degeneration.
Dosage & Administration: 1 teaspoon of Amla powder with water or raw honey, or 15-20 ml fresh juice in morning.
Contraindications: Highly tolerated; reduce dosage if causing mild loose stools.

GUDUCHI / GILOY (Tinospora cordifolia)
Known as 'Amrita' (nectar of immortality), Guduchi is a premier tridoshic immunomodulator that detoxifies the blood and liver.
Properties: Bitter, pungent, and astringent rasa; heating virya, sweet vipaka; balances all three doshas, specially Pitta and Vata.
Therapeutic Benefits: Purifies the blood (Raktashodhaka), eliminates metabolic toxins (Ama pachana), regulates immune response, reduces chronic fever, and supports liver and kidney function.
Dosage & Administration: 1-3 grams of powder or 15-30 ml decoction (Kwath) taken twice daily before meals.
Contraindications: Generally very safe; monitor blood sugar if combined with diabetic pharmaceuticals.

HARIDRA / TURMERIC (Curcuma longa)
Turmeric is a foundational spice and medicine balancing all three doshas, especially Kapha and Vata, while non-aggravating to Pitta when used correctly.
Properties: Pungent and bitter rasa; warming virya; pungent vipaka.
Therapeutic Benefits: Powerful natural anti-inflammatory, purifies blood and skin, enhances bile flow, eases joint stiffness and pain, and protects digestion from Ama accumulation.
Dosage & Administration: 1-3 grams with warm milk (Golden Milk) and a pinch of black pepper (Maricha) and ghee for bioavailability.
Contraindications: Avoid excessive high doses in acute gallstones or acute bleeding disorders.

NEEM (Azadirachta indica)
Neem is the bitter cooling purifier of Ayurveda, celebrated for its blood-cleansing and skin-rejuvenating prowess, particularly pacifying Pitta and Kapha.
Properties: Bitter and astringent rasa, cooling virya, pungent vipaka; strongly pacifies Pitta and Kapha.
Therapeutic Benefits: Clears skin eruptions, acne, eczema, and rashes; supports healthy blood sugar levels; purifies blood (Rakta dhatu); clears intestinal parasites.
Dosage & Administration: 250-500 mg powder or capsules after meals with warm water for 2-4 weeks at a time.
Contraindications: Highly cooling and drying; avoid during pregnancy, in extreme Vata depletion, emaciation, or for continuous long periods without breaks.
